Cognitive Tasks (Objective Assessment)

Purpose: Brief objective measures of executive function that are harder to fake than self-reports. Administer verbally during assessment.

Digit Span Backward (Working Memory)

Read digits at ~1/second. Patient repeats backward. Start with 2 digits, increase until failure.

Norm: 4-6 digits typical

Letter-Number Sequencing

Read mixed letters/numbers (e.g., "B-5-A-2"). Patient sorts: "2-5-A-B". Increase difficulty until failure.

Norm: 3-5 items typical

Trail Making Test (Described)

Part A: Connect 1-25 in order. Part B: Alternate 1-A-2-B-3-C... Record time in seconds.

Norms: Part A <29s, Part B <75s (age 18-39)

Cluster A: Inattention

A1 Attention to Detail / Careless Mistakes
"Do you often fail to give close attention to details, or do you make careless mistakes in your work or during other activities? Do you find that work is inaccurate or you overlook details?"
Adult: Work poorly presented/rushed; misses details in emails/forms; works slowly to avoid errors.
Child: Careless mistakes in schoolwork; left questions unanswered; didn't check answers.
A2 Sustaining Attention
"Do you often have difficulty sustaining your attention in tasks or play activities? Do you find it hard to stay focused during lectures, conversations, or long readings?"
Adult: Quickly distracted by own thoughts; difficulty watching a whole movie or reading a book; bored easily.
Child: Difficulty keeping attention on schoolwork or play; needed structure to avoid distraction.
A3 Listening When Spoken To
"Does it often seem as though you are not listening when you are spoken to directly? Do others complain that your mind seems 'elsewhere'?"
Adult: Dreamy or preoccupied; forgets what was just said; changes subject frequently; only listens with eye contact.
Child: Parents/teachers said "you're not listening"; didn't know what the teacher said; dreamy.
A4 Follow Through / Finishing Tasks
"Do you often not follow through on instructions and fail to finish schoolwork, chores, or duties in the workplace? Do you start tasks but quickly lose focus?"
Adult: Starts tasks but gets sidetracked; difficulty with administrative tasks; needs a deadline to finish.
Child: Difficulty following instructions with multiple steps; not completing homework; unfinished projects.
A5 Organizing Tasks
"Do you often find it difficult to organize tasks and activities? Do you struggle to manage your time or keep your belongings in order?"
Adult: Disorganized home/office; double-booking appointments; poor time management; does things in a muddled way.
Child: Messy room/desk; difficulty planning homework; arrived late; needed others to structure things.
A6 Avoidance of Mental Effort
"Do you often avoid, dislike, or are reluctant to engage in tasks that require sustained mental effort (like preparing reports, completing forms, or reviewing long papers)?"
Adult: Procrastinates on boring/difficult tasks; avoids administration; does easiest tasks first.
Child: Avoided homework; resisted reading books; avoided tasks requiring concentration.
A7 Losing Things
"Do you often lose things that are necessary for tasks or activities (e.g., wallet, keys, paperwork, eyeglasses, mobile phone)?"
Adult: Spends a lot of time searching for things; panics if things are moved; leaves things behind.
Child: Lost school materials, clothes, or toys; parents/teachers commented on lost items.
A8 Distractibility
"Are you often easily distracted by extraneous stimuli (like noise or movement) or unrelated thoughts?"
Adult: Distracted by conversations of others/background noise; difficulty filtering information; distracted by own thoughts.
Child: Looking out the window in class; easily distracted by noises; difficulty picking up the thread after distraction.
A9 Forgetfulness
"Are you often forgetful in daily activities (e.g., doing chores, running errands, returning calls, paying bills, keeping appointments)?"
Adult: Forgets appointments/obligations; needs frequent reminders/lists; returns home to fetch forgotten items.
Child: Forgot instructions; forgot to take things to school; left things at friends' houses.

Cluster B: Hyperactivity & Impulsivity

H1 Fidgeting
"Do you often fidget with or tap your hands or feet, or do you squirm in your seat?"
Adult: Difficulty sitting still; tapping pens/feet; playing with hair/objects; biting nails; inner restlessness.
Child: Parents said "sit still"; rocking on chair; kicking legs; fiddling constantly.
H2 Leaving Seat
"Do you often leave your seat in situations where it is expected that you remain seated (e.g., meetings, office, church, classroom)?"
Adult: Walks around rather than sitting; makes excuses to leave meetings; avoids sedentary situations.
Child: Standing up while eating; wandering around classroom; difficulty staying seated at school/meals.
H3 Restlessness
"Do you often feel restless? (In children: running about or climbing in situations where it is inappropriate.)"
Adult: Feeling agitated/restless inside; hard to relax; constantly feeling the need to "do something."
Child: Running/climbing furniture; "climbing the walls"; excessive energy.
H4 Quiet Leisure
"Do you often find it difficult to engage in leisure activities quietly?"
Adult: Talks during movies/TV; loud in public; difficulty doing activities quietly.
Child: Loud during play/school; unable to watch TV quietly; becoming too loud/rowdy in public.
H5 On the Go / Driven by Motor
"Is it often difficult for you to be 'still' for an extended time? Do you feel 'on the go' or act as if 'driven by a motor'?"
Adult: Always busy; uncomfortable being still; others find you exhausting to keep up with; stepping over boundaries.
Child: Constantly busy; excessively active at school/home; always on the move.
H6 Excessive Talking
"Do you often talk excessively?"
Adult: Known as an incessant talker; difficulty stopping talking; dominating conversations; needing many words to say something.
Child: Known as a chatterbox; punished for talking in class; keeping others from work by talking.
H7 Blurts Out Answers
"Do you often blurt out an answer before a question has been completed? Do you complete people's sentences?"
Adult: Saying things without thinking; finishing others' sentences; being tactless; "blabbermouth."
Child: Answering before the teacher finishes asking; being the first to answer; interrupting others.
H8 Difficulty Waiting Turn
"Do you often find it difficult to await your turn (e.g., in lines, traffic, conversation)?"
Adult: Jumping queues; impatience in traffic; impatience in relationships/jobs.
Child: Difficulty waiting in games/groups; always wanting to be first; crossing road without looking.
H9 Interrupting or Intruding
"Do you often interrupt or intrude on others (e.g., butting into conversations, games, or activities)?"
Adult: Interfering with others; taking over tasks; using others' things without asking; comments from others about interference.
Child: Interrupting games; using others' toys without permission; interrupting conversations.
